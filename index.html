What's the guarantee exactly? +
We guarantee that you only pay for calls with qualified prospects. If they don't show, you don't pay.
How much of my time is required? +
Very little. We handle everything up to the call. You just need to show up, pitch, and close. Onboarding takes around 3–4 hours in the first week. And we have a quick strategy session once a week to review your results and make adjustments.
Will this work for me? +
Cold outreach can work for any B2B business that sells a high-ticket service or product. Agencies, consultants, SaaS companies, coaches, and more. If there's a defined ICP, we can build a system around it.
Do you charge any additional fees? +
We charge a tech fee which is used to cover the cost of all the tools and infrastructure needed to run your cold outreach campaigns. This is a recurring monthly fee that scales with volume. This cost would be needed even if you were to run cold email yourself.
